Comprehensive Sheet Metal Solutions

AMADA provides "comprehensive solutions” for sheet metal industry, through integrated, network intelligent CNC machines, software products and services to manufacture sheet metal parts for industry segments like infrastructure, telecom, electronics, home/office equipment, retail businesses and gaming consoles, to name a few.

Major product lines of Amada include Sheet Metal Processing, Structural Steel Processing, Stamping Press Machinery, and Metal Cutting Machine Tools.
